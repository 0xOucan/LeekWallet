Passphrase

A passphrase opens a separate wallet from the same seed. The device forgets it on lock, on a wallet switch and on disconnect.

Enter it on the device. Its own keyboard is slower — a long passphrase is a few hundred button presses — and it is the only way the passphrase never leaves the device at all.

Type it here instead (less safe)

Typing a passphrase here is weaker than typing it on the device, and the encrypted link does not change that. This app sees every character before anything is encrypted, so anything that has this computer — malware, a screen recorder, a keyboard app that syncs what you type — sees it too. On a phone, your keyboard is part of that risk.

A wrong passphrase does not fail. It opens a different, empty wallet. The device shows the first address of whichever wallet it derived: check that address before you use it, and confirm on the device.
